## Life CLI

```bash
atlas life init [--repo OWNER/atlas-life]
atlas life wake | remember | recall | rollup | pull | push | sync
```

Life palace: `$ATLAS_LIFE_ROOT` or `~/atlas-life`. GitHub **private** only. Mind Map only (no Graphify). Wake = today + hot set. Remember → drawers under `day/YYYY-MM-DD`. Desktop auto-push after remember.
